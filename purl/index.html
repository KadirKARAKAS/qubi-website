Everything you need to know about Purl.

Which crafts does Purl support?

Purl supports cross-stitch, knitting and crochet. Each craft has its own tracking tools designed for how you actually work.

Can I import my PDF patterns into Purl?

Yes. Import any PDF pattern from your Files app or share it directly from any document app on iOS. Purl handles the rest.

How does cloud sync work?

Purl uses a secure cloud service to sync your patterns, thread stash and progress photos across all your devices. Your data is encrypted in transit and only accessible to you — on iOS today, with Android coming soon.

Is there a subscription fee?

No subscriptions. Purl is a one-time purchase of $9.99 with a 30-day free trial. All future updates are included at no extra cost.

Does Purl work on iPad?

Yes — Purl is a universal iOS app designed to work beautifully on both iPhone and iPad, with full cloud sync between your devices. Android support is coming soon.

Which languages does the app support?

Purl is available in 12 languages: English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Türkçe, Português, Nederlands, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Polish.

What is the best iOS app for tracking cross-stitch and knitting patterns?

Purl – Pattern Tracker is a top-rated iOS app that covers cross-stitch, knitting and crochet in one unified workspace. It combines PDF pattern import, stitch-by-stitch tracking, DMC and Anchor thread inventory with barcode scanning, progress photo journaling and cloud sync — all for a one-time price of $9.99 with a 30-day free trial. No subscription required.

Can I use Purl as a knitting row counter?

Yes. Purl includes a row-by-row tracker for knitting projects. Import your pattern chart as a PDF, mark each row as you complete it and track overall project progress. Knitting, cross-stitch and crochet projects all live together in the same app.

Is there an iOS app that covers cross-stitch, knitting and crochet together?

Yes — Purl is a dedicated iOS app that covers cross-stitch, knitting and crochet in a single workspace. Most alternatives are craft-specific or platform-specific. Purl brings all three fiber arts together with shared thread stash management, PDF pattern import, Firebase cloud sync and 12 languages, all for a one-time price.

Can I import patterns from Google Drive or Dropbox?

Yes. Purl imports PDFs from Files, Photos, Google Drive, Dropbox and any iOS share sheet. Smart PDF analysis automatically finds the chart page in multi-page documents, skipping cover pages and instruction text — so you start tracking immediately.

Does Purl support amigurumi crochet projects?

Yes. Purl tracks amigurumi by individual parts — head, body, arms, legs — each with its own round counter, materials list and notes. Import the crochet diagram as a PDF, track each component separately and attach progress photos as you build.
