Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026  ·  Qubi LLC

01

Overview

Purl – Pattern Tracker ("App") is developed and operated by Qubi LLC. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (when you sign in with email/password)
  • Authentication tokens (managed securely by Firebase Auth)
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, or payment card details

Content You Create

  • Cross-stitch, knitting, and crochet project data (title, notes, stitch progress, completion status)
  • PDF pattern files you import (stored encrypted in Firebase Storage under your account)
  • Progress photos you upload within the App
  • Thread/colour key configurations

Purchase Information

  • Whether you have purchased the app (stored as a boolean flag locally on your device)
  • Purchase verification is performed via RevenueCat. RevenueCat may collect transaction identifiers for receipt validation. We do not store your Apple ID or payment details.
  • See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy

Usage Data (Automatically Collected)

  • Crash reports and error logs (via Firebase Crashlytics, if enabled)
  • Basic app diagnostics (device type, iOS version, app version)
  • We do NOT track individual user behaviour, screen flows, or analytics beyond what is necessary to keep the App functioning

Device Permissions

  • Camera / Photo Library: only accessed when you explicitly add a progress photo
  • Notifications: only if you enable stitch reminders in Settings
  • Local Storage: PDF patterns and app preferences are cached locally for offline access
03

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide and sync your cross-stitch, knitting and crochet projects across your devices
  • Restore your purchase and verify your subscription entitlements
  • Deliver stitch reminder notifications you have opted into
  • Improve app stability through crash reports
  • Respond to your support requests

We Do Not

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Share your project content, photos, or PDF patterns with anyone
  • Build advertising profiles from your usage
04

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Google LLC), which provides industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Firebase servers are located in the United States.

Access to your data is restricted by Firebase Security Rules — only you (authenticated with your account) can read or write your own projects, photos, and PDF files. Purl staff cannot access your content without your explicit consent.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account within the App, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Delete your account and all associated data from within the App (Settings → Account → Delete Account), or by contacting us
  • Portability — Request an export of your project data
  • Objection — Object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@qubillc.com with the subject line "Purl Privacy Request".

For EEA/UK users: if you believe we have not addressed your concern, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, request deletion of personal information, and opt out of the sale of personal information.

We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at info@qubillc.com.
