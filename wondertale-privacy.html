This Privacy Policy explains how personal data and device data are collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use the WonderTale – Kids Stories app.

Our app may provide content intended for children. For this reason, we place importance on data minimization, security, and parental transparency.

1) Data We Collect

We may collect a limited amount of data necessary for the app to function.

A) Account and Identity Data

Anonymous User ID (UID): Automatically created through Firebase Authentication.

Purpose: Account status, progress, usage limits, and subscription/function matching.

B) App Usage and Content Data

  • Story preferences and in-app selections, such as category preferences, favorites, and reading progress.
  • Character creation data, such as selected items and generated character images.
  • User-generated content, such as text/prompts entered for AI story generation and, where applicable, uploaded story content.

Purpose: To provide app features, enable cross-device synchronization, and personalize the experience.

C) Purchase / Subscription Data

Subscription and purchase status, including premium access, trial status, and entitlement information.

Processing: Through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.

Purpose: Purchase verification, unlocking premium features, and subscription management.

D) Technical, Diagnostic, and Analytics Data

  • Device and app technical information, such as operating system, app version, language/locale, and limited technical metadata.
  • Error/crash logs required for security and performance.

Limited app usage events may be collected through Firebase and Google Analytics for Firebase. These events are limited to general app interactions such as app opens, screen views, story creation, audio playback start, subscription screen views, trial starts, and purchase process starts.

We may also use Meta App Events / Facebook SDK to measure the effectiveness of app promotion campaigns and understand limited app interactions related to app installs and in-app engagement. Events shared with Meta are limited to general app interactions, such as app activation, story creation, audio playback start, subscription screen views, trial starts, and purchase process starts.

We do not send children’s names, email addresses, usernames, story text, AI prompts, precise location, phone numbers, birth dates, user IDs, advertising IDs, or sensitive personal information as analytics or Meta App Events parameters.

Purpose: Troubleshooting, security, performance improvement, understanding app usage, and measuring advertising campaign effectiveness.

E) Advertising and Tracking Identifiers (Important)

Our app does not contain third-party ads.

To protect children’s privacy, advertising identifiers are not used for behavioral advertising or personalized ad targeting.

Google Analytics for Firebase, Meta App Events / Facebook SDK, and similar infrastructure may process limited technical identifiers or app instance information for app performance, basic usage analytics, and advertising campaign measurement. This information is not used to show personalized ads to children or to track children for behavioral advertising purposes.

Advertising identifiers, children’s names, emails, story text, AI prompts, user IDs, or sensitive personal data are not sent as analytics or Meta App Events parameters.

Meta App Events are used only for limited campaign measurement and app event reporting. We do not use Meta App Events to send children’s personal information, story content, AI prompts, or sensitive data to Meta.

Data We Generally Do Not Collect

We do not collect unnecessary sensitive data such as precise location, contacts, SMS content, call logs, or biometric data unless required by an app feature.

2) How We Use Data

We use collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide the core functions of the app, such as story display, personalization, saving progress, and account continuity.
  • To improve the user experience.
  • To manage subscriptions and premium access.
  • To prevent abuse, security issues, and technical problems.
  • To analyze app performance, errors, and general usage flows.
  • To measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and evaluate app promotion efforts.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3) Legal Bases for Processing Data

For GDPR and Similar Regulations

Depending on your country, we may rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: To provide the app’s functions.
  • Legitimate interests: Security, fraud prevention, technical improvement, basic usage analytics, and campaign performance measurement.
  • Legal obligation: Accounting, tax, and legal requirements.
  • Consent, where required: For certain operations that require permission.

4) Third-Party Service Providers

The app may use the following infrastructure and service providers:

Google Firebase

Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Functions, Cloud Messaging, Google Analytics for Firebase, and related services.

Privacy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Analytics for Firebase

May be used to understand app usage, measure basic analytics events, improve performance, and evaluate advertising campaign effectiveness. Analytics events are limited to general app interactions, and children’s names, emails, story text, prompts, precise location, or sensitive personal information are not sent as analytics event parameters.

Privacy: policies.google.com/privacy

Meta App Events / Facebook SDK

May be used to measure the effectiveness of app promotion campaigns and understand limited app interactions related to app installs and engagement. Meta App Events may include general app interactions such as app activation, story creation, audio playback start, subscription screen views, trial starts, and purchase process starts.

We do not send children’s names, email addresses, usernames, story text, AI prompts, precise location, phone numbers, birth dates, user IDs, advertising IDs, or sensitive personal information to Meta as event parameters.

We do not use Meta App Events to show personalized ads to children or to track children for behavioral advertising purposes.

Privacy: facebook.com/privacy/policy

Google Play Billing

Privacy: policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat

Subscription management.

Privacy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

Texts used in the story generation process, such as inputs/prompts, may be transmitted to our service providers, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar providers, in an anonymized manner. This data is not linked to your personal identity and is not used to train AI models.

These providers process data only for the purpose of providing their services and in accordance with their own agreements and policies.

5) Children’s Privacy (Important)

WonderTale – Kids Stories may be intended for child users. Therefore:

  • We apply data minimization.
  • We do not request unnecessary personal information from children.
  • We prioritize child safety in relation to advertising and tracking activities.
  • We do not use children’s data for behavioral advertising or personalized ad targeting.

Analytics events collected through Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase and limited events measured through Meta App Events are used only to understand general app interactions, technical usage data, and advertising campaign effectiveness.

Children’s names, email addresses, story text, AI prompts, precise location, user IDs, advertising IDs, or sensitive personal information are not sent as analytics or Meta App Events parameters.

We prioritize requests from parents or legal guardians.

If you believe that personal data belonging to a child has been processed inappropriately, please contact us at: info@qubillc.com.

6) Data Retention Period

Data is retained only for as long as necessary:

  • Account/progress data: For as long as the account is active or as long as required to provide the service.
  • Purchase records: For the period required under legal and financial obligations.
  • Technical logs and analytics data: For a reasonable period necessary for troubleshooting, security, performance improvement, usage analysis, and campaign performance measurement.

After the retention period, data is deleted, anonymized, or access is restricted.

7) Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect data, including:

  • Authentication and authorization controls,
  • Encrypted network communication using TLS/HTTPS,
  • Access restrictions and logging mechanisms,
  • Security measures provided by infrastructure providers.

No system is 100% secure; however, we follow industry best practices to reduce risks.

8) International Data Transfers

Due to the cloud infrastructure we use, data may be processed on servers located outside your country. In such cases, appropriate safeguards are applied in accordance with applicable laws.

9) User Rights

Depending on local laws, such as KVKK/GDPR, you may have the following rights:

  • Access to your data,
  • Correction,
  • Deletion,
  • Restriction of processing / objection,
  • Data portability, where applicable,
  • Withdrawal of consent, where processing is based on consent.

For rights requests, contact us at: info@qubillc.com. For security purposes, we may request identity verification.

10) Account and Data Deletion Request

To request deletion of app data, you may contact us:

  • Email: info@qubillc.com
  • Subject: “WonderTale – Kids Stories Data Deletion Request”
  • Additional information: In-app user ID/device information, if possible.

We will process your request within the time required by applicable laws.

11) Links to Other Websites / Content

The app may contain links to third-party web pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these pages. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of the relevant service.

12) Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be published in the app or on our official website. We will notify users of significant changes where appropriate.

13) Contact

For any privacy-related questions:

Uygulamanın çalışması için sınırlı düzeyde veri toplayabiliriz.

A) Hesap ve Kimlik Verileri

Meta ile paylaşılan olaylar; uygulama etkinleştirme/açılış, hikaye oluşturma, ses oynatma başlatma, abonelik ekranı görüntüleme, deneme başlatma ve satın alma süreci başlatma gibi genel uygulama etkileşimleriyle sınırlıdır.
